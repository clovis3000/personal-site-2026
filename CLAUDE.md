# lewisthompson.dev

Lewis's personal site, shown to people deciding whether to hire him. Since 2026-10-20 it is the illuminated site: medieval maximalism (Morris pattern, gold leaf, acanthus, a calendar arch) held against Japanese and Swiss modernism (flat geometric planes, tracked capitals, a white ground, generous space). The seams it lives on: a katagami stencil (one geometric plane in one ink with Morris cut out of it), mon and heraldry (each project's gold emblem is its logomark), emaki cloud bands and the scroll itself. Text is DOM; one WebGPU canvas behind it paints the ornament, placed by measuring the DOM's layout ("the DOM is the scribe, the GPU is the illuminator"). It was developed in `~/code/frontend-explorations/incipit` (plate 15, `15-emaki/`), where the plates the making-of page plays were made; `site/` is the deploy root as it ships. The previous, Japanese-modernist site is kept whole in `archive/`, with its own CLAUDE.md beside it.

## Anti-patterns

- **Don't lift a source image's composition.** The first version's cover was Design no. 163's circles with "logic / human / agent" pinned to them. Lewis read it as his thesis forced onto a borrowed graphic (2026-10-11). Take the process, the principle and the colour, then make the form from what the page has to say.
- **Don't dress the page as a magazine.** Issue numbers, plate numbers, fig. labels, eyebrows, runheads and captions read as costume (2026-10-11, again 2026-10-20 for the MAGI insert). The making-of page's lines are sentences, not captions.
- **Don't add Japanese text.** It was removed on 2026-10-11, and the fonts carry no CJK glyphs, so a character falls back silently to a system face. Plate 10, played inside the making-of page, leaves out its 間 when given `?embed` for this reason; keep that guard if the plate is replaced.
- **Don't reference Neon Genesis Evangelion in MAGI's figure or copy.** The MELCHIOR / BALTHASAR / CASPER labels came out at Lewis's request (2026-10-11).
- **Don't let motion keep performing after the entrance.** Sections paint once as they arrive and hold. What may keep going: gold leaf catching the pointer, MAGI's packets while its insert is on screen, and the one plate playing in the making-of reel. A new loop at full amplitude is a regression.
- **Don't run a frame loop that never stops.** `site.js` draws only while a clock, the track or the light is still moving; MAGI's canvas runs only while its sheet intersects the screen; the reel keeps at most one plate's iframe alive and removes it when the page closes. Each of these is a GPU kept awake on a visitor's laptop if it leaks.
- **Don't gate content on JavaScript.** Entrance offsets are scoped under `.js`; the `<noscript>` block in `index.html` turns the two sliding columns into one document with every project page shown. Check it whenever a page or a hidden state is added.
- **Don't measure the layout mid-entrance.** The painting is placed from `getBoundingClientRect`; measured while `.text > *` were still translated 16 px for their entrance, every mon landed 16 px low (2026-10-20). `compose()` sets `.measuring`, which lifts the offsets; anything new that animates position must be lifted there too.
- **Don't let the index's `section` grid style a block inside a project page.** The bare `section` rule made the MAGI insert a twelve-column grid and squeezed it into one column (2026-10-20). New blocks reset `display`, `min-height` and `padding`.
- **Don't make a GPU-painted element `position: sticky` and expect the painting to follow.** Strokes live in world space. The seafaring chart pins because it has its own world column with a camera read from the sticky element every frame (`Bristles.cams`); do the same for anything else that pins.
- **Don't time a section's strokes past its own span.** The index's clock stops at the end of the newest revealed section, so anything section `i` paints after `(i + 1) * SPAN` waits, unseen, until the next section is scrolled to: MAGI's amber thread was a stub and the hero's blossom never opened (2026-10-20). Only the last page gets extra time.
- **Don't edit `frontend-explorations/incipit/15-emaki` expecting it to ship.** `site/` has moved on from it since the port (2026-10-20); it stays as the sketchbook the making-of page describes.
- **Don't let a stroke cross a world column boundary.** A segment is drawn with the camera of the column its first point is in; one that starts at `x < vw` and ends past it shows up in the wrong place during the slide (the hero's right-edge vine, 2026-10-20).
- **Don't route a sea path by its endpoints alone.** Straight or bowed routes between sites crossed Qatar, Oman and Saurashtra (2026-10-20). Routes are waypoint lists over open water, and `seaMap` warns in the console when one spends more than a tenth of its length on land.
- **Don't present the seafaring chart as the model's output.** Its coast is approximate and its scores come from a toy drift, blind to the sites as the real study was. The page says it is a sketch of the method; keep saying so.
- **Don't leave anything in `site/` that shouldn't ship.** `just deploy` mirrors it with `--delete`. Scratch work (the display-face specimen, plate debug pages) stays in frontend-explorations; old versions go in `archive/`.
- **Don't size full-height blocks in `vh`.** Use `svh`, so the mobile URL bar showing and hiding doesn't resize the canvas on every scroll.
- **Don't run `just fonts` without checking it against the new faces.** `tools/fonts.py` subsets Archivo and Newsreader for the old site; Inter and JetBrains Mono (MAGI's insert) were copied in from MAGI's build unsubsetted.
- **Don't ship `og.png` as it is.** It is the old site's triangle card (2026-10-20); regenerate it from the new hero before or with the first deploy.
