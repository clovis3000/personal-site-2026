# lewisthompson.dev

Lewis's personal site, shown to people deciding whether to hire him. Since 2026-10-20 it is the illuminated site: medieval maximalism (Morris pattern, gold leaf, acanthus, a calendar arch) held against Japanese and Swiss modernism (flat geometric planes, tracked capitals, a white ground, generous space). The seams it lives on: a katagami stencil (one geometric plane in one ink with Morris cut out of it), mon and heraldry (each project's gold emblem is its logomark), emaki cloud bands and the scroll itself. Text is DOM; one WebGPU canvas behind it paints the ornament, placed by measuring the DOM's layout ("the DOM is the scribe, the GPU is the illuminator"). It was developed in `~/projects/graphic-explorations/incipit` (plate 15, `15-emaki/`), where the plates the making-of page shows recordings of were made; `site/` is the deploy root as it ships. The previous, Japanese-modernist site is kept whole in `archive/`, with its own CLAUDE.md beside it.

## Anti-patterns

- **Don't lift a source image's composition.** The first version's cover was Design no. 163's circles with "logic / human / agent" pinned to them. Lewis read it as his thesis forced onto a borrowed graphic (2026-10-11). Take the process, the principle and the colour, then make the form from what the page has to say.
- **Don't dress the page as a magazine.** Issue numbers, plate numbers, fig. labels, eyebrows, runheads and captions read as costume (2026-10-11, again 2026-10-20 for the MAGI insert). The making-of page's lines are sentences, not captions.
- **Don't add Japanese text.** It was removed on 2026-10-11, and the fonts carry no CJK glyphs, so a character falls back silently to a system face. Plate 10, recorded for the making-of page, leaves out its 間 when given `?embed` for this reason; keep that guard if the plate is replaced or re-recorded.
- **Don't reference Neon Genesis Evangelion in MAGI's figure or copy.** The MELCHIOR / BALTHASAR / CASPER labels came out at Lewis's request (2026-10-11).
- **Don't let motion keep performing after the entrance.** Sections paint once as they arrive and hold. What may keep going: gold leaf catching the pointer, MAGI's packets while its insert is on screen, the one recording playing in the making-of reel, and the last page's caelum turning while its arch is on screen and the index is home (2026-11-02, ported from incipit's plate 24; it runs on the index's clock, which is why that clock runs on past the last beat). A new loop at full amplitude is a regression.
- **Don't run a frame loop that never stops.** `site.js` draws only while a clock, the track or the light is still moving; MAGI's canvas runs only while its sheet intersects the screen; the reel keeps at most one video alive and unloads it when the page closes. Each of these is a GPU kept awake on a visitor's laptop if it leaks.
- **Don't gate content on JavaScript.** Entrance offsets are scoped under `.js`; the `<noscript>` block in `index.html` turns the two sliding columns into one document with every project page shown. Check it whenever a page or a hidden state is added.
- **Don't move the phone breakpoint in one place only.** The phone has its own composition: `paint()` paints it when `vw <= 820` and the CSS lays it out under `max-width: 820px` (in `site.css` and in `gate/gate.css`). If they disagree, ornament is painted for a layout the text isn't in.
- **Don't give the gate (`site/gate/`) a relative path.** nginx serves it, with a 401, at whatever URL under `/for/<slug>/` was asked for, so `gate.css` resolves against the client's demo and 401s too. Its HTML and CSS use root paths; its module imports are relative to `gate.js`, which is safe.
- **Don't put client demos in `site/`, or the demos' nginx anywhere but `tools/nginx/`.** `just deploy` mirrors `site/` with `--delete`; demos live in the droplet's `/var/www/lewisthompson-demos` and go up only through `tools/demos.sh` (the `client-demo` skill drives it), whose `setup` installs `tools/nginx/` (2026-11-03).
- **Don't put the demos' rate limit back beside the `return 401`.** `if ... return` runs in nginx's rewrite phase, before `limit_req`, so a limit there never counted a wrong guess (found testing, 2026-11-03); it lives on `@demo_gate`, which every refusal passes through.
- **Don't give anything inside a `section` the class `text`.** `.js section .text > *` is the index's entrance and holds its children at opacity 0 until the section is revealed; the making-of reel's closing card was invisible because of it (2026-10-20).
- **Don't compose or lay out the painting on the main thread.** Done there, the index took 2.7 s before anything showed, the words included, and each project page stalled the page for most of a second (2026-10-20). `site.js` only measures the page into a plan; `paint.js` paints from the plan in `paint-worker.js`, and the page uploads what comes back. So `paint.js` must never touch the DOM: a `document` or `$` in it breaks the worker silently into the slower fallback.
- **Don't gate the words on the painting.** `reveal()` runs as soon as the faces are in, before the GPU starts; the painting's clocks wait for their strokes instead, so nothing jumps ahead when they arrive.
- **Don't measure the layout mid-entrance.** The painting is placed from `getBoundingClientRect`; measured while `.text > *` were still translated 16 px for their entrance, every mon landed 16 px low (2026-10-20). `measureIndex()` sets `.measuring`, which lifts the offsets of sections not yet in (the hero's entrance is already playing by then, and lifting it would cut it short); anything new that animates position must be lifted there too.
- **Don't let the index's `section` grid style a block inside a project page.** The bare `section` rule made the MAGI insert a twelve-column grid and squeezed it into one column (2026-10-20). New blocks reset `display`, `min-height` and `padding`.
- **Don't make a GPU-painted element `position: sticky` and expect the painting to follow.** Strokes live in world space. The seafaring chart pins because it has its own world column with a camera read from the sticky element every frame (`Bristles.cams`); do the same for anything else that pins.
- **Don't time a beat's strokes past its own span.** The index's clock runs in beats, and stops at the end of the newest one revealed, so anything beat `k` paints after `(k + 1) * SPAN` waits, unseen, until the next beat is scrolled to: MAGI's amber thread was a stub and the hero's blossom never opened (2026-10-20). Only the last beat gets extra time. A section is one beat, or two where its art is stacked under its words (`measureBeats()`, 2026-11-02); strokes of the art's half start from `Ta`, the words' from `T`.
- **Don't start a section's art on its words' beat when it is stacked below them.** On a phone, Pum's circle sits most of a screen under its heading, and painted while still off screen: it had finished by the time anyone had read the blurb (2026-11-02). A new stroke beside a plane takes `Ta`.
- **Don't edit `frontend-explorations/incipit/15-emaki` expecting it to ship.** `site/` has moved on from it since the port (2026-10-20); it stays as the sketchbook the making-of page describes.
- **Don't let a stroke cross a world column boundary.** A segment is drawn with the camera of the column its first point is in; one that starts at `x < vw` and ends past it shows up in the wrong place during the slide (the hero's right-edge vine, 2026-10-20). Ornament may run off the index's *left* edge (a column below 0 clamps to the index's own), never its right: the phone's arch bleeds left for this reason, and its gear moves right to stay in view (2026-11-02).
- **Don't route a sea path by its endpoints alone.** Straight or bowed routes between sites crossed Qatar, Oman and Saurashtra (2026-10-20). Routes are waypoint lists over open water, and `seaMap` warns in the console when one spends more than a tenth of its length on land.
- **Don't present the seafaring chart as the model's output.** Its coast is approximate and its scores come from a toy drift, blind to the sites as the real study was. The page says it is a sketch of the method; keep saying so.
- **Don't put the live plates back in the reel.** They ran in same-origin iframes, which share the page's main thread, and each built its whole painting before its first frame: Meander blocked the scroll for 630 ms, Ma and Folio for 400 (2026-10-20). The reel plays H.264 recordings instead; they are made by `incipit/record.html` and `incipit/record.sh` in frontend-explorations, so don't encode them by hand.
- **Don't serve `site/` locally with plain `python3 -m http.server`.** It sends no Cache-Control, and Chrome kept a stale `paint-worker.js` through hard reloads: the caelum painted but never turned, because the old worker didn't pass its spin groups back (2026-11-02). `just serve` (`tools/serve.py`) sends `no-store`.
- **Don't leave anything in `site/` that shouldn't ship.** `just deploy` mirrors it with `--delete`. Scratch work (the display-face specimen, plate debug pages) stays in frontend-explorations; old versions go in `archive/`.
- **Don't size full-height blocks in `vh`.** Use `svh`, so the mobile URL bar showing and hiding doesn't resize the canvas on every scroll.
- **Don't run `just fonts` without checking it against the new faces.** `tools/fonts.py` subsets Archivo and Newsreader for the old site; Inter and JetBrains Mono (MAGI's insert) were copied in from MAGI's build unsubsetted.
- **Don't let `og.png` drift from the hero.** The first port shipped the old site's triangle card by default (2026-10-20). It is now a capture of the hero's own painting: `__site.draw()` and a `drawImage` of `__site.page.canvas` in the same task, cropped and set beside the name in Archivo. Recapture it when the hero changes.
