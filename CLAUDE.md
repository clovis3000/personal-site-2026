# lewisthompson.dev

Lewis's personal site, shown to people deciding whether to hire him. It carries the same content as 3rigby.xyz (stance, pum, Project MAGI, the triangle thesis, contact). Its ancestry is 1960s–70s Japanese modernist graphic design (`mood -b inbox` and `mood -b japanese-modernism`, where Lewis's ratings are the brief), taken as principles and colour rather than images: airbrush grain inside crisp geometry, flat inks on paper, asymmetric balance, generous space. The aim is harmony through simplicity, in a form that belongs to the web. The opening image is his thesis's triangle drawn as the GPU's first triangle (a colour at each vertex, blended across the face), airbrushed. `site/` is the deploy root as it ships.

## Anti-patterns

- **Don't lift a source image's composition.** The first version's cover was Design no. 163's circles with "logic / human / agent" pinned to them. Lewis read it as his thesis forced onto a borrowed graphic (2026-10-11). Take the process, the principle and the colour, then make the form from what the page has to say.
- **Don't dress the page as a magazine.** Issue and volume numbers, dates, "issued from", fig. labels, eyebrows and runheads, registration marks and type credits were all removed on 2026-10-11. The synthesis is a web page, and that apparatus reads as costume.
- **Don't add Japanese text.** It was removed on 2026-10-11 for the same reason. The fonts no longer carry CJK glyphs either, so any character would also fall back silently to a system face.
- **Don't reference Neon Genesis Evangelion in MAGI's figure or copy.** The MELCHIOR / BALTHASAR / CASPER labels came out at Lewis's request (2026-10-11). The figure is about the product: a page of the record, marked spans, and threads to entities.
- **Don't let motion keep performing after the entrance.** The brief (2026-10-11) asks for movement that settles into subtle life, because the work and the thesis are the substance. Everything lands in about 2.5 s. After that, only these keep going: the triangle's slow breathing, its pour and its lean toward the pointer, the logomark's slower pour, the jellyfish's sway, the MAGI caret and the diagram packets. A new effect that loops at full amplitude is a regression, however good it looks on its own.
- **Don't start a `requestAnimationFrame` loop outside `actor()`.** The shared loop stops when nothing visible needs a frame. Once settled, the triangle draws at 24 fps (enough for a 9 px/s drift to read as moving sand) and the logomark at 10 fps, and only while it's shown. A free-running loop keeps the GPU awake on every visitor's laptop for as long as the tab is open, and nothing on the page shows it happening.
- **Don't render a sprayed or stochastic surface below device resolution, and don't scale one.** The airbrush is a per-pixel random threshold. Resampling it turns droplets into mud, the same law 3rigby learned with its dither. Canvases are sized `css × min(dpr, 2)`. og.png is captured at DPR 1 for the same reason: never downscale a DPR 2 capture.
- **Don't animate the triangle with discrete events.** Comets travelling the edges were tried on 2026-10-11 and replaced: Lewis wanted a general, continuous movement, "like three pourings of sand". Each ink's grain field drifts away from its corner instead.
- **Don't move the grain by re-randomising it, and don't let the pour change coverage.** Sand reads as sand only if each grain field translates coherently; re-rolled grains read as TV static. Each ink's threshold is conditioned on the inks before it, so it still covers exactly `D × w` of the face, and the drift wraps at the grain's 1024 px tile so it never loses float precision.
- **Don't repaint the footer bands in the triangle's inks.** Tried on 2026-10-11 for cohesion: pink, pale aqua and light tints in horizontal stripes read as the trans pride flag, which isn't what the page is saying. The bands keep their own greens, blues and earths (after Graphic Design 17), as literal stops, the one deliberate exception to the rule below.
- **Don't write an ink as a literal in the script, and don't move one vertex ink on its own.** The shader, the canvases and the SVG figures read `:root` custom properties through `ink()`, so a colour lives in one place. `--logic`, `--human` and `--agent` are both the opening triangle's corners and the thesis diagram's vertices, and the reader is meant to recognise them. favicon.svg and og.png are files and can't read the custom properties: regenerate them when a vertex ink moves.
- **Don't gate content on JavaScript.** Every pre-animation hidden state is scoped under `.js`, `prefers-reduced-motion` renders the final frame, and the triangle has a CSS fallback for no JS, no WebGL or a lost context. A hiring reader on a locked-down browser must still get every word.
- **Don't leave anything in `site/` that shouldn't ship.** `just deploy` mirrors it with `--delete`. A shader prototype sat in `site/_proto` during the 2026-10-11 redesign; keep scratch work outside the deploy root.
- **Don't ask Newsreader for another weight or Archivo for another width without unpinning the axis in `tools/fonts.py`.** Both are pinned to cut each load by ~200 KB. The browser synthesises the missing style and says nothing.
- **Don't size the opening in `vh`.** It uses `svh` so the mobile URL bar showing and hiding doesn't resize the WebGL canvas on every scroll.
