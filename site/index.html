SOFTWARE IS A TRIANGLE

Software in the post-AI era has three vertices: the business logic, the human, and the agent.

Interaction and representation occur at the edges, the interfaces, and every edge runs both ways. Synthesis occurs at the vertices. The agent vertex is a crucible: it reads what the logic produces, holds what it knows of the person, and pattern-matches the two into something neither could produce alone.

An application becomes a context factory. It captures, structures and exports precise data about its domain, and hands it to the assistant the user already trusts. Without the agent it’s still a good tool. With it, it becomes something much larger.

Most software still builds one edge and calls it finished.
